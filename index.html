    What is Phishing?

    Phishing is a cyberattack where attackers try to trick people into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, banking details or personal information.

    Things to look for:

    • Suspicious or unfamiliar domains
    • URLs using HTTP instead of HTTPS
    • IP addresses instead of domain names
    • Suspicious words such as "login" or "verify"
